# Retrospective Formats

Rotate formats every 2–3 sprints to keep retros useful. Every retro, whatever the format, ends with **one to three owned actions** that have a due date.

## Retro skeleton (60 minutes, any format)

| Time | Step | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Set the stage | Check-in, review last retro's actions |
| 10 min | **Look at data** | Run `backlog_health.py` and review the sprint metrics |
| 20 min | Gather insights | Use the format below |
| 15 min | Decide | Dot-vote, then pick 1–3 actions |
| 10 min | Close | Assign owners and dates, rate the retro |

## Formats

### 1. Start / Stop / Continue
*Best for: new teams, and a fast default.*
Three columns. Simple and action-oriented.

### 2. 4Ls: Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed for
*Best for: after a release or milestone.*
Captures emotion and learning, not only process.

### 3. Sailboat
*Best for: seeing risks and blockers.*
- 🏝 Island: the goal
- 💨 Wind: what pushes us forward
- ⚓ Anchor: what slows us down
- 🪨 Rocks: risks ahead

### 4. Quality Gate Review ⭐ *(toolkit-specific)*
*Best for: teams with rework, escaped defects, or carry-over.*

For every story that was reopened, carried over, or produced a defect, ask:

| Story | What went wrong | Which gate should have caught it? | Why didn't it? | Gate change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | G1 / G2 / G3-DoR / G4-DoD / G5 | | Add a check / automate / clarify |

The output is a concrete edit to the DoR, DoD, or linter rules. The system gets better every sprint.

### 5. Flow Retro
*Best for: slow delivery or unpredictable throughput.*
Map the last three stories' actual path through the board, with the days spent in each column. Circle the longest waits. Wait time, not work time, is usually the problem.

### 6. Mad / Sad / Glad
*Best for: after a hard sprint, or when morale is low.*
