## Tailoring by audience

The data is the same for every audience. What changes is how you present it.

| Audience | Show | Hide |
|---|---|---|
| Executives | Outcomes, metrics, fixed dates, risks | Stories, estimates |
| Business stakeholders | Outcomes, epics, what changes for them and when | Technical enablers |
| Delivery team | Everything, including enablers and dependencies | — |
| Customers / partners | Themes and "coming soon" benefits | Internal metrics, anything uncommitted |
